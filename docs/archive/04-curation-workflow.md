# Phase 4 — Curation Workflow Specification

> **Superseded (2026-09-19).** Inclusion must be fully automated with no human review queue.
> One-time calibration lives in `01a-discovery-calibration.md`; ongoing quality checks in
> `01-discovery-strategy.md` §11. Kept only for reference; parts of §6 (feedback reports to UWPR)
> may be reused later.

**Status:** Unreviewed starting point · 2026-09-19
**Purpose:** define how people review uncertain candidates, how their decisions are recorded,
and how findings flow back to UWPR.

## 1. Why this phase exists

Phase 1 measured that roughly a third of the papers on UWPR's own list carry no machine-findable
trace of the resource. Discovery for those works ends in a judgement call by someone who knows
the facility. The workflow must make that judgement **quick, recorded once, and permanent**.

## 2. Roles

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Reviewer (UWPR staff) | Decides queue items; adds works nobody can find automatically |
| Maintainer | Tunes channels, roster and search terms in response to review outcomes |
| Pipeline | Builds the queue, applies decisions, never alters them |

## 3. The review queue

Generated by `uwpr-pubs report` after every run as `reports/review_queue.csv` and a browsable
`reports/review_queue.html`. One row per **work family** that is:

- tier 2 or tier 3 with no decision; or
- decided, but new evidence has appeared since the decision; or
- a possible duplicate / uncertain family link; or
- an official-list entry the pipeline could not resolve to any identifier.

Each row carries what a reviewer needs to decide without leaving the page: title, year, venue,
authors with staff and roster PIs highlighted, links (DOI, PubMed, PMC), nominating channels,
every evidence excerpt, full-text check status, and a suggested decision.

**Ordering** (so limited reviewer time goes furthest): tier 2 before tier 3; then by a score
built from number of independent channels, roster-PI frequency in the confirmed corpus, UW
affiliation, proteomics topic match, and recency.

**Volume control.** Broad channels (G, H) can nominate thousands. Tier-3 items below a score
threshold are held in a backlog, not shown, and counted in the report. The threshold is tuned
from measured precision.

## 4. Recording decisions

Decisions live in `curation/decisions.yaml` (Phase 2 §5.5). Two ways to write them:

1. **CLI:** `uwpr-pubs review next` shows one item at a time and appends the decision.
   `uwpr-pubs review decide F-000210 include --reason "…"` for direct entry.
2. **Bulk:** fill the `decision` and `reason` columns in the CSV, then
   `uwpr-pubs review import reports/review_queue.csv`.

Values: `include`, `exclude`, `probable` (keep visible as probable), `defer` (ask again in N
days, e.g. pending a reply from the PI). A reason is mandatory for `include` and `exclude`.

**Rules**

- Decisions are permanent inputs. The pipeline never edits or removes them.
- New evidence never silently overturns a decision; the item is re-queued with the new evidence
  shown next to the old decision.
- `exclude` decisions double as negative fixtures and as training signal for channel tuning.
- Decisions target families; if families are later merged or split, decisions follow the
  canonical record and conflicts are queued.

## 5. Manual additions and PI confirmation

- `curation/manual_additions.yaml` holds works known to staff that no channel finds. Each needs
  an identifier, a reason and a reviewer.
- For roster PIs with many tier-3 candidates, the report can produce a per-PI list suitable for
  emailing: "Which of these used the Proteomics Resource?" Replies are recorded as decisions with
  `reason: "confirmed by PI, <date>"`. This is the only route to the papers that never mention
  UWPR, and it has the side benefit of reminding users to acknowledge the resource.

## 6. Feedback to UWPR

Each run produces `reports/missing_from_official_list.md`: tier-1 families not on the UWPR
publications pages, formatted in the site's own entry style so they can be pasted in. Likewise
`reports/official_list_issues.md`: preprint/article duplicates, entries lacking a PMID or link,
and entries whose link does not resolve.

## 7. Feedback to discovery

After each review session the maintainer looks at `uwpr-pubs audit precision`:

- a channel with low precision gets a tighter `confirm` step or a lower tier;
- recurring wording found in `include` decisions is added to `search_terms.yaml`;
- PIs appearing repeatedly in `include` decisions are added to `roster.yaml`.

Config changes trigger a full run (Phase 3 §6).

## 8. Initial curation pass

Before launch, a one-time pass is needed:

1. Triage the ~150 not-on-list candidates already found (Phase 1 §2).
2. Verify staff author IDs in `staff.yaml`.
3. Review official-list entries with no resolvable identifier (8 today).
4. Confirm family links proposed for the official list's internal duplicates.

Estimated effort to be measured on the first 50 items.

## 9. Open questions

1. Who reviews, and how much time per run is realistic? (D5)
2. Is emailing PIs acceptable, and from whom? Default: optional, initiated by UWPR staff.
3. Should a second reviewer confirm `exclude` decisions on tier-2 items? Default: no.
4. Is a local HTML review page enough, or is a shared (hosted) review tool needed? Default:
   local files + git; revisit if more than one reviewer.

## 10. Exit criteria

- [ ] Queue columns, ordering score and backlog threshold agreed.
- [ ] Decision vocabulary and rules agreed.
- [ ] Reviewer identified; initial pass scheduled.
